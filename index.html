About SLV Granites

Crafting Spaces with the Finest Granite, Marble & Tile

With over 20 years of experience, SLV Granites is a trusted name in premium natural stone and surface solutions. We deal in a wide range of granites, marbles, and tiles, offering quality products to suit residential, commercial, and architectural needs.

  • Retail Showroom: Direct-to-consumer model focused on selling finished slabs and tiles to homeowners, local builders, and interior designers.
  • Wholesale Trading: Sourcing bulk quantities directly from processing units and distributing them to smaller retail dealers across regions.
  • The sustained demand for granite spans across residential, commercial, and public infrastructure projects.
